Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a showcase site and a web application?

A showcase site presents an activity: who you are, what you do, how to contact you. A web application does something: calculation, form, dashboard, management, tracking. I build both.

Will my site work on a phone?

Yes. Every site is designed to stay clean on all screens, from a small phone to a computer.

Can you put my site online and handle the domain name?

Yes. I publish the site on the suitable platform and explain the options before you decide, including any hosting and domain fees.

Can you redo my existing site?

Yes, that is a redesign: new design, better speed, correct display on mobile.

Do you work from a Figma mockup?

Yes. I faithfully convert the mockup into production-ready pages.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the number of pages, the features and the deadline. I do not publish a fixed rate: describe your project on WhatsApp and you will receive a quote.
