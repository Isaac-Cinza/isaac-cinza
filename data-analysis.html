What I do

  • Data preparation

    Coding, cleaning, handling missing values and structuring the dataset.

  • Descriptive analysis

    Frequencies, means and cross tables.

  • Statistical analysis

    Chi-square, t-test and ANOVA to test differences and associations between variables.

  • Advanced analysis

    Logistic and linear regression, multivariate analysis.

  • Visualization and dashboards

    Charts, tables and dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, Excel), ready to include in a report.

  • Digital data collection

    Questionnaires on KoboToolbox, Google Forms or Microsoft Forms, tested before fieldwork, with structured data export.

  • Interpretation in plain language

    Statistical results explained clearly so that everyone understands and can decide.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to have data already?

No. If you do not have any yet, I can build the questionnaire and the digital collection (KoboToolbox, Google Forms, Microsoft Forms), then analyze the data collected.

Which files can you work with?

Excel and CSV files, as well as files from analysis software (SPSS, Stata, R). If your dataset is in another format, write to me and I will tell you how to prepare it.

How much does an analysis cost?

The price depends on the amount of data, the complexity of the analysis and the deadline. I do not publish a fixed rate: describe your need on WhatsApp and you will receive a quote.

Will my data stay confidential?

Confidentiality is agreed before work starts, together with you. Please do not send anything sensitive before discussing it with me.

Can you teach me to analyze my own data?

Yes, that is the purpose of the data analysis training, with the tool of your choice, including Excel.

I am preparing a dissertation: is this the right service?

Analyzing the data of a dissertation is part of it. For framing the topic, methodology and the defense, see research consulting.
