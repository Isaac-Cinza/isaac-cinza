Frequently asked questions

Will you write my dissertation for me?

No. I guide and structure: topic, method, analysis, outline, methodological review, defense preparation. The work stays yours, which is what makes it defensible in front of your jury.

Which kinds of work do you support?

Dissertations, final-year projects, theses and scientific articles.

Can you help me choose my topic?

Yes. We start from your interests, and from the needs and gaps observed in the field, to find a relevant and feasible topic.

Do you check plagiarism and AI use?

Yes, as part of quality control: plagiarism, AI-generated content, citations and references. It is to help you hand in clean work, not to get around your university's rules.

I live in another country: is it possible?

Yes. Support is provided remotely, in French or in English.

How much does the support cost?

The price depends on the work requested and the deadline. I do not publish a fixed rate: describe your need on WhatsApp and you will receive a quote.
